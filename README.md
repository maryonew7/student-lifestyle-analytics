# Student Lifestyle Analytics

## Overview
Analysis of a 100-student dataset (Aug 2023 – May 2024) examining how 
study hours, sleep, extracurricular activity, and physical activity 
relate to GPA and stress levels.

## Contents
- Data Collection & Exploration
- Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Data Visualization (Pie, Bar, Scatter charts)
- Statistical Analysis (Correlation & Linear Regression)
- Insights & Business Recommendations

## Key Findings
- Study hours show a very strong positive correlation with GPA (r = 0.81)
- High stress levels are the most common category among students
- Sleep hours show negligible correlation with GPA
